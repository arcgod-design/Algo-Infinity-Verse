Premature Convergence

If you run the Exponential schedule on the "Trap" scenario, watch it closely. It cools down too fast, driving the Acceptance Probability to near 0%. It will get stuck in a suboptimal local minimum and stay perfectly frozen while the others escape.

📜

Hajek's Theorem

The Logarithmic schedule guarantees finding the global optimum as time approaches infinity. Because T decays so slowly, the algorithm maintains enough thermal energy to escape ANY local minimum. It's theoretically perfect, but incredibly slow in practice.
